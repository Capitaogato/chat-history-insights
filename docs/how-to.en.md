# Your AI usage report – how it works

**You need:** a computer, your ChatGPT account and about 30 minutes.

1. **Download the file** `chat-history-insights.py`. You don't need to open it. It is a plain text file for ChatGPT.
2. **Open ChatGPT** and start a new chat.
3. **Drag the file into the chat window** and add this sentence:

   > **Create my AI usage report with the attached file.**

4. **Send.** From here, ChatGPT guides you step by step, including how to export your chats.

The conversation follows your language. You get the report in English or German, just tell ChatGPT which one you prefer.
